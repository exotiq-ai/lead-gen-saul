# Exotiq Operator Phone Outreach Playbook

Updated: 2026-05-22
Owner: Gregory Ringler
Purpose: convert enriched operator leads into real conversations without sounding like generic SaaS sales.

## Core principle

This is not a demo pitch. The first call is a founder-to-operator fit check.

Goal of the first call:
1. Verify they are a real ICP operator.
2. Learn how they run bookings, pricing, fleet ops, insurance/compliance, and follow-up today.
3. Identify an obvious pain point.
4. Earn permission for a deeper 15-minute look or in-person coffee if they are high-value.

## Routing

### Score 5, 25+ vehicles or major-market/multi-location operator
Phone first. Gregory only.

### Score 4, 15-24 vehicles or strong-market operator
Phone or email first depending on phone quality. Gregory can call the best ones. Team can prep notes.

### Score 3, 8-14 vehicles
Email/IG/website form first, call if phone is clean or the operator looks especially active.

### Score 1-2
Hold unless there is a special reason.

## 30-second opener, Score 5

Hey [Name], this is Gregory Ringler. I run Exotiq, we build fleet management tools for exotic rental operators.

I’ll be quick. I saw [specific proof point: fleet size, city, multi-location, high-end cars]. I’m talking with a few operators in [market] about how they’re handling pricing, bookings, and fleet ops right now.

Not trying to pitch you cold. I wanted to see if it’s worth comparing notes for five minutes.

## 30-second opener, standard operator

Hey [Name], Gregory Ringler here with Exotiq.

I came across [Company] while looking at exotic rental operators in [market]. Looks like you’re running [specific proof point]. I used to operate in this space, and we’re building the command center I wish existed when I was managing cars, bookings, and customer follow-up manually.

Quick question, are you still handling most bookings through text/DMs, or do you have a system you like?

## If gatekeeper answers

Totally understand. I’m not calling about renting a car. I’m Gregory Ringler, founder of Exotiq. We build software for exotic rental operators, and I’m trying to connect with whoever handles fleet operations or bookings.

Who would be the best person for that?

## If they say “send me info”

Absolutely. I’ll send a short note.

Before I do, what’s the one thing that would actually be useful to see, booking workflow, pricing automation, fleet calendar, or marketplace/direct booking side?

## Discovery questions

Use 4-6 max. Do not interrogate. Pick based on the flow.

### Operations
1. How are you managing fleet availability right now, spreadsheet, rental software, Google Calendar, something custom?
2. Where do most bookings come from today, Instagram, Google, repeat customers, referrals, Turo, brokers, website?
3. What happens after someone texts or DMs you asking for a car?
4. How many people touch a booking before it is confirmed?
5. What part of the booking process still feels too manual?

### Pricing
6. How are you setting rates today, fixed daily rate, gut feel, events/seasons, or competitor checks?
7. Do you adjust pricing for events, weekends, tourism spikes, or last-minute demand?
8. If demand doubles in a given weekend, would your pricing automatically catch that or would someone have to notice?

### Fleet and utilization
9. Roughly how many cars are active right now?
10. Which cars are easiest to keep rented, and which sit more than they should?
11. Do you know utilization by vehicle without manually checking calendars or payment records?
12. Are you trying to grow fleet size this year or improve margin on the current fleet?

### Insurance / compliance
13. How are you handling driver verification, deposits, insurance checks, and agreements today?
14. Any recurring issues with claims, bad renters, chargebacks, or documentation?
15. If a renter damages a car, how easy is it to pull the full paper trail?

### Direct booking / marketplace
16. Are you trying to get more direct bookings instead of relying on third parties?
17. How much of your business still comes through Turo or broker/referral channels?
18. If there were a marketplace focused only on premium exotic operators, would you want early access or would that not matter?

### Buying signal questions
19. If you could fix one operational bottleneck this month, what would it be?
20. What would make this worth looking at, more bookings, less admin, better pricing, cleaner compliance, or something else?
21. If this saved your team 10 hours a week or improved utilization, who would need to see it?

## Qualification score during call

Score A, high priority:
- 15+ cars, or high-end fleet with growth plans.
- Current workflow is fragmented.
- Owner/founder is involved.
- Wants direct bookings or better pricing/utilization.
- Open to seeing product.

Score B, worth nurture:
- 8-14 cars.
- Some pain, but less urgency.
- Not sure who decides.
- Needs follow-up proof.

Score C, hold:
- Under 5 cars.
- Hobby operator.
- No growth intent.
- Mainly Turo side hustle.
- Not independent or not relevant.

## Soft close options

### If there is pain
That’s exactly the kind of workflow we’ve been building around. Worth grabbing 15 minutes next week so I can show you what this looks like with an operator workflow, not a generic SaaS dashboard?

### If they are high-value / Score 5
I’d rather not give you a generic pitch. If you’re open to it, I can put together a quick operator-specific view and show you where I think this could help [Company]. Worth a quick look next week?

### If they are skeptical
Completely fair. I’m not asking you to switch anything today. I’m trying to learn from serious operators and show you what we’ve built. If it’s not useful, you’ll know in five minutes.

### If they ask price early
Depends on fleet size and how deep the rollout is. For the operators we’re onboarding early, we’re more focused on fit and outcomes first. If it looks useful, we can talk numbers after you’ve seen it.

## DO NOT SAY

- Do not say “book a demo.”
- Do not say “beta operators.”
- Do not lead with discounts.
- Do not lead with “AI tools.”
- Do not overpromise marketplace or insurance as fully live if context is early/founding-operator.
- Do not sound like an SDR.
- Do not ask to “pick your brain.”
- Do not say “just following up.”
- Do not call Gregory “Greg.”

## Notes to capture after each call

Minimum:
- Decision maker name
- Best phone/email
- Fleet size, confirmed or estimated
- Current booking workflow
- Current pricing workflow
- Current insurance/compliance workflow
- Primary acquisition channel
- Pain level, low/medium/high
- Direct booking interest, low/medium/high
- Marketplace interest, low/medium/high
- Next step
- Follow-up date
- DO NOT SAY / sensitive context
